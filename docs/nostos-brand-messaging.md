# Nostos — Brand Messaging

## Brand identity

**Name:** Nostos  
**Pronunciation:** `NOS-tos`  
**Root:** Greek *nostos*, meaning a return, homecoming or journey back  
**Category:** RWA redemption and settlement infrastructure  
**Descriptor:** Clear exits for tokenized real-world assets  
**Primary tagline:** **Capital on its way home.**

The name is a strategic asset, not a literal feature label. It creates a memorable territory around the product’s central promise: capital should have a visible, reliable way home after redemption.

Trademark, domain, social-handle, cultural and legal clearance have not been performed.

## Executive diagnosis

Nostos should not lead as a generic RWA yield aggregator. Yield discovery is useful, but the sharper and more ownable problem is the exit experience:

- the user clicks redeem;
- the tokens disappear;
- capital becomes temporarily unusable;
- the settlement state is unclear;
- the user cannot easily sell the pending claim;
- the issuer often provides limited visibility.

Nostos makes that exit visible, trackable and optionally liquid.

> **Nostos makes RWA yield easier to enter and safer to leave.**

The product’s most important distinction is:

> **Availability tells you that an asset can be bought. Nostos helps you understand how and when you can leave.**

Nostos gives RWA vaults that missing exit layer. It connects a visible asynchronous queue with a controlled instant-liquidity path and records important state transitions on BOT Chain.

## Verification Receipt language

The public record should be readable without understanding the entire protocol:

```text
NOSTOS SETTLEMENT / 0041

VAULT          RWA DEMO VAULT
REQUEST        #0041
PATH           INSTANT CASHOUT
GROSS          10,000 USDT
DISCOUNT       0.30%
NET            9,970 USDT
STATE          CLAIM TRANSFERRED
CHAIN          BOT MAINNET
TRANSACTION    VIEW EXPLORER
```

### Receipt rules

- show the vault before the amount;
- show the request ID;
- distinguish standard settlement from instant cashout;
- show gross amount, discount and net amount;
- show whether the claim has transferred;
- show whether the underlying has settled;
- show the chain and explorer transaction;
- distinguish estimated information from confirmed information;
- never call a claim “cash” before the relevant transaction is confirmed.

## Objection responses

### “Why not simply sell the token?”

> Selling the underlying token may expose you to market price movement, slippage or limited liquidity. Nostos gives you a separate choice: keep the standard redemption path or sell the defined pending claim at a disclosed discount when liquidity is available.

### “Is instant cashout free?”

> No. The discount is the visible cost of receiving liquidity before standard settlement. You see the gross amount, discount and net amount before confirming.

### “Is this risk-free?”

> No financial infrastructure is risk-free. Nostos makes the queue, settlement terms, liquidity capacity and transaction history more visible so users can make a better-informed decision.

### “Does Nostos guarantee the underlying yield?”

> No. The underlying issuer and asset determine the yield, backing and eligibility terms. Nostos provides the discovery, redemption and settlement experience around those assets.

### “What happens if there is no instant liquidity?”

> The standard settlement path remains available. The interface clearly shows when instant liquidity is unavailable rather than implying that every request can be cashed out immediately.

### “Why BOT Chain?”

> BOT Chain gives the redemption record a public on-chain home. Nostos uses it to record requests, state changes and settlement receipts rather than keeping the exit experience inside a private issuer dashboard.

Every major message should pass the following tests:

- Can a stranger understand what Nostos is within five seconds?
- Does the message distinguish yield discovery from redemption infrastructure?
- Does it show who the product is for?
- Is the standard settlement time visible?
- Is the instant-cashout discount visible?
- Does it distinguish a pending claim from cash?
- Is the settlement state explicit?
- Is the BOT Chain role clear?
- Does the message avoid universal safety or guaranteed-yield claims?
- Can a user explain Nostos as “the clear exit layer for tokenized assets”?

## Messaging tests to run

### Landing-page comprehension test

Show the hero to people unfamiliar with Nostos and ask:

1. What does this product do?
2. Who is it for?
3. What happens when someone wants their money back?
4. What does instant cashout cost?

If they answer “it is a generic yield platform,” the message is failing.

### Redemption-choice test

Show standard settlement and instant cashout side by side. Test whether users can accurately repeat:

- how long standard settlement may take;
- how much instant cashout costs;
- what happens to the pending claim;
- whether liquidity is guaranteed.

### Trust test

Remove the marketing copy and show only the receipt. Ask whether the user can identify:

- the vault;
- the request;
- the amount;
- the state;
- the discount;
- the transaction;
- what is still pending.
